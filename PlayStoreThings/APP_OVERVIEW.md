# Kids Quiz: what the app does

*Updated 27 November 2026*

**Kids Quiz** is a learning app for children aged **3 to 10**, from **LKG and UKG to Class 5**. A child learns letters, numbers, maths, science, GK, English and Hindi through picture quizzes, stories, rhymes, games and Olympiad practice papers. A friendly owl guides them.

- **Android app:** `com.mk.kidsquiz` on Google Play
- **Website:** mayankkhandelwal1991.github.io/kidsQuiz (the same app, in a browser)
- **Price:** free, with child-safe ads and optional in-app coins (no real money)
- **Languages:** English, plus Hindi quizzes, Hindi stories and Hindi rhymes

---

## 1. The first time a child opens the app

1. **Name:** the child types a first name (letters only, no surname). A parent can instead sign in with Google, behind a grown-up question.
2. **Three questions, read aloud by the owl:**
   - *What would you like to learn?* Pick any: ABC & Phonics, Numbers & Maths, Stories & Rhymes, Science & Nature, English & Words, Hindi, Drawing & Music, Puzzles & Brain games.
   - *Which class are you in?* LKG, UKG, Class 1–5.
   - *Would you like to play and learn with your friends?*
3. **Home:** the home page is built from these answers. The child or a parent can change the answers any time from the plan card ("✏️ Change").

Brothers and sisters each get their own profile, with their own answers, stars and progress (see section 9).

---

## 2. The home page

From top to bottom:

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Header** | The child's name and picture, level, and today's points, accuracy and play time |
| **Today for you** | The owl's 3-step plan for today, plus any tasks from the child's teacher |
| **Learn & Play** | The four main areas: **Quiz**, **Games**, **Friends** (online games), **Olympiad** |
| **Pick a skill** | One tap into a skill. The child's interests come first: Stories, Rhymes, Read, Create, Move, ABC, Phonics, Tracing, Counting, Colours, Shapes, Tables, Spelling, Maths, Science, Brain |
| **Fun zone** | Daily gift, question of the day, learning map, weekly league, badges, coin shop, weekly report |
| **For parents** | Progress, settings, children's profiles, teacher mode, rate and share (behind a grown-up question) |

A bottom bar jumps to Home, Quiz, Skills, Stories, Play, Fun and Rank (leaderboard).

### Today's plan (personal and adaptive)

Every day the owl makes 3 steps for the child, from their answers and how they are doing:

1. **A story**, or drawing or a brain puzzle for children who picked those.
2. **A quiz:**
   - first, the subject where the child is weakest (under 70%);
   - otherwise, a subject they like that they haven't tried;
   - after a very good run (90%+), a **harder class**; after a hard time (under 40%), an **easier class**.
3. **A fun step:** play with a friend (every other day, if the child likes friends), times tables, drawing, a puzzle, or letter tracing.

The owl can read the plan aloud (🔊).

### Guide Owl

The owl appears on the home page only, never inside a game, quiz or story. The rest of the screen dims so its message is clear. The owl:

- says good morning once a day and suggests something new;
- suggests the child's own interests first;
- after a child finishes a few quizzes or games, says "Great playing!" and introduces a feature they haven't used yet: leaderboard, question of the day, daily gift, Fun zone, weekly league, badges, learning map, coin shop or Play with friends.

It shows at most 3 tips per visit and 8 per day, and "Later" hides that idea for the day.

## 3. Quiz (20+ subjects)

Each quiz "paper" has **10 questions** and gives stars and points.

**LKG & UKG (pre-readers): picture quizzes read aloud**

Counting, Numbers, Colours, Shapes, ABC, Phonics, Animals, **Who Says? (real animal sounds)**, Fruits, Vehicles, Body, Think, Patterns, Weather, Good Habits, English, Hindi, GK. UKG adds simple add & subtract, Space and Science.

- Every question is spoken, with big pictures, big text and big answer buttons, so children who can't read yet can play alone.
- **Who Says?** plays real recordings of 15 animals (cow, cat, dog, lion, duck, hen, sheep, horse, pig, frog, owl, bee, goat, monkey, mouse). The sound credits are on the privacy page.

**Class 1 to Class 5**

Add & Subtract, Multiplication, Division, Mixed Maths, English, Hindi, GK, Science, History, Geography, Sports, Computers, Animals, Plants, Human Body, Space, Capitals, Riddles, Patterns, Reasoning. Questions match each class, at NCERT level.

The class picker always opens first, so a child can choose their class (and "⭐ My class" is marked).

---

## 4. Olympiad practice papers

For **Class 1 to Class 5**, in 8 subjects: Mathematics, Science, English, Computer, GK, Social Studies, Reasoning and Creative Arts.

- 5 papers per subject, each with 20 questions and 25 marks, including "Achievers" questions.
- A timer, a score, and a full **answer review** (correct answer, wrong choice, missed).
- The best score per paper is saved.

## 5. Story Time: stories and rhymes told like a storyteller

**57 picture stories** and **21 rhymes & songs**, for every class from LKG to Class 5.

- **Story kinds:** animal tales, moral stories, Indian tales (Birbal, Tenali Raman), feelings, good habits, science, adventure, funny, bedtime, easy readers, and some stories where **the child is the hero, by name**.
- **Rhymes:** classic English rhymes, Hindi rhymes (e.g. *Machli Jal Ki Rani Hai*) and original learning songs (days of the week, months, planets, water cycle, body, India).

**How a story is told:**

- **Opening:** the storyteller says hello by name: *"Hello Aarav! Come, sit close. Today's story is… The Hare and the Tortoise."*
- **Voices:** a warm narrator, and characters in their own voices (a deep, slow lion; a squeaky mouse; a bright child).
- **Highlighting:** each sentence is highlighted and each word lights up as it is read. Tapping a word says it again.
- **Sounds:** real animal sounds, splash, wind, magic chimes, rain, jumps.
- **Pictures:** the character being talked about glows and gets a 💬 speech bubble. The camera zooms slowly, and scenes are alive (butterflies, birds, falling leaves, jumping fish, twinkling stars).
- **Mid-story question:** halfway, the storyteller asks *"What do you think will happen next?"*.
- **Ending:** 3 questions about the story, which the child can **skip**.
- **Rhymes:** each rhyme has its **own sing-along music** (Hindi rhymes get dholak and flute). Lines come on the beat, everyone in the picture dances, hands clap, and animals make their real sounds (Old MacDonald's cow moos).
- **Reading:** stories play by themselves, or page by page with ⏸ / ▶.

## 6. Skills and activities

| Activity | What the child does |
|---|---|
| **Letter Tracing** | Trace A–Z, a–z and 0–9 with a finger. It only finishes when the letter is really traced (centre line and every part). |
| **Phonics** | Letter sounds, "which picture starts with b?", sounding out c-a-t |
| **Times Tables** | Tables 2–20 and mixes, with a **sing-along chant** of the table, then 10 questions and 3 stars per table |
| **Read (sight words)** | Dolch sight words, as word cards and a listening game |
| **Creative corner** | Colouring pages, free drawing and a xylophone |
| **Move & feelings** | "Hop like a frog, stretch like a giraffe" movement breaks and feelings activities |
| **Spelling Bee** and word games | Spelling, missing letter, word builder, rhyme time, word search, word ladder, hangman, category sort |

## 7. Games (54 single-player games)

Every game has a "how to play" card, stars, a best score and a leaderboard.

- **Puzzle (10):** 2048, Sudoku Mini, Slide Puzzle, Memory Match, Hidden Objects, Water Sort, Stack Tower, Tetris-style blocks, Bubble Shooter, Connect Four
- **Word (9):** Letter Tracing, Spelling Bee, Word Builder, Word Search, Word Ladder, Missing Letter, Rhyme Time, Hangman, Category Sort
- **3D (6):** Car Race, Endless Runner, Crossy Road, Ball Maze, Basketball, Snake 3D
- **Strategy and more (8):** Tic Tac Toe, Rock Paper Scissors, Simon Says, Ant Attack, Target Gallery, Feed the Monster, Train the Robot (a first look at AI)

## 8. Play with Friends (24 online games)

Children play with friends on other phones using a **room code**. There is **no chat**; players see only nicknames and game moves.

Tic Tac Toe, Four in a Row, Rock Paper Scissors, Word Duel, Typing Speed Race, Memory Match, Reaction Duel, Trivia Battle, Dots & Boxes, Battleship, Ping Pong Duel, Space Shooter Duel, Blaster Arena, Hoops Shootout, Bubble Shooter Duel, Archery Duel, Snake Duel, Wordle Duel, Whack-a-Mole, Doodle Guess, Math Blitz, Ludo Royale, Trivia Party, Red Light Green Light.

## 9. Rewards that keep children coming back

- **Points and levels:** every quiz and game adds points, and the child moves up levels (Starter and up).
- **Coins:** earned by finishing quizzes and games, and spent in the **coin shop** on fun looks. The app owner can switch coins on or off. When a child has too few coins to start, they can watch one short ad and play, or play anyway when no ad is ready. A child is never blocked.
- **Badges:** for streaks, totals and milestones.
- **Daily gift** and **question of the day**.
- **Learning map:** a path of stars to win.
- **Weekly league:** a friendly weekly ranking in small groups.
- **Leaderboards:** for every game and quiz (first names only).
- **Certificates:** a Weekly Star Report to share.
- **Daily goal:** 15 minutes of play.

## 10. Family: one phone or one Google account, many children

- **Profiles:** up to **6 child profiles** per phone. Each child has their own name, picture, class, answers, stars, coins and leaderboard row.
- **"Who is playing?"** appears when the app opens. Children switch without signing out.
- **With Google sign-in**, the children list is saved with the parent's account, so **every phone signed in with that Gmail shows the same children**. Each child keeps their own scores.
- **Changing a profile:** a parent can rename a child and change their picture or class. Removing a child only hides them; their scores stay saved.

## 11. For parents and teachers

**Parents** (behind a grown-up question):

- **Progress:** per subject, what needs practice, and counts of quizzes, games and stories.
- **Daily time limit:** when time is up, a grown-up can add 15 minutes.
- Manage children's profiles, sound, rating and sharing.

**Teacher mode:**

- A teacher (signed in with Google) creates a **class code**.
- Children join with the code in Fun zone → League.
- The teacher gives a **quiz or a story** as homework. It appears in the child's **Today for you**.
- The teacher sees who finished it and their score, by first name only.

## 12. Child safety, privacy and ads

Built for Google Play's **Families** programme (under 13):

- **Names:** first names or nicknames only (letters only, up to 12). Leaderboards show first names only, never a full Google name or photo for brothers and sisters.
- **No chat** anywhere. In Doodle Guess, only words from the game's word list are shown to other players.
- **Grown-up question** before sign-in, links outside the app, rating, sharing and parent settings.
- **Ads:** child-directed and family-safe, with no advertising ID. Full-screen ads show only **after** a quiz or game, with gaps between them, never in the middle of play. The app goes **completely quiet while an ad is open**. There are no rewarded-video ads.
- **Rating:** the Google Play rating sheet is shown rarely, after a few finished activities.
- **Privacy policy:** in the app and on the website, including sound credits.

## 13. How it is built (for the team)

- **Web app:** plain HTML/JavaScript, hosted on GitHub Pages. Every change goes live without a new app release. Script version numbers (`?v=`) make phones load new code.
- **Android app:** a WebView shell (`android/`) that adds the phone's read-aloud voice (`Android.speak` / `Android.speakEx` for character voices), AdMob ads, Google sign-in and the In-App Review sheet.
- **Firebase:** Realtime Database for leaderboards, play history, coins, leagues, the children list, teacher classes and tasks; Firebase Auth for Google sign-in. The rules are kept in `admin/firebase-rules.json` (not in git).
- **Content as data:**
  - stories in `data/stories.json` and rhymes in `data/rhymes.json`;
  - quizzes in `quiz/` (generated per class);
  - Olympiad papers in `Olympiad/data/`.
- **Music:** made live in the browser (`games/kq-chant.js`), so there are no music files to download.
- **Admin console** (`admin/`):
  - dashboard: active players, new players, top games, countries;
  - players list, grouped by family;
  - coins, app settings (ads, rating, minimum app version) and tools.
