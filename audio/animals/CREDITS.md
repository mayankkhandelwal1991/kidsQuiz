# Animal sounds — credits

Real recordings used in the “Who says?” quiz (quiz/kids-engine.js → k_sounds).
Each clip was shortened to its loudest 1–4 seconds, turned into mono and made louder (normalised); nothing else was changed.
The shortened clips keep the original licence. The snake “ssss” is made by the app (no recording).
