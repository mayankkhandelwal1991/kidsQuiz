Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Kids Quiz & Game ("App", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Android application.

Kids Quiz & Game is developed and published by Mayank Logic. This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application Kids Quiz & Game (com.mk.kidsquiz).

About Our App

Kids Quiz & Game is an educational application designed for children and families. The Android application securely displays content from our official website using WebView technology.

Picture-based questions use emoji graphics from Twemoji, Copyright 2020 Twitter, Inc and other contributors, licensed under CC-BY 4.0.

Login Options

Users may access the app using:

  • Guest Login
  • Google Sign-In

Guest Login allows users to play without signing in. If a user chooses Google Sign-In, Google Firebase Authentication is used to securely authenticate the user.

Information We Collect

Depending on how the app is used, we may collect:

  • Name (Google Sign-In only), or a nickname typed by a guest player
  • Email Address (Google Sign-In only)
  • Google Profile Picture (if available)
  • Firebase User ID (UID), or a random guest ID for guest players
  • Game progress, quiz scores and play history (which quizzes and games were played, scores, date and time, time spent)
  • Coins, badges (achievements), streaks, items bought in the in-app owl shop with coins, and the child's school class (e.g. "Class 3") chosen in the app
  • Country (a two-letter code such as "IN"), worked out on the device from the SIM / network or the time zone — we do not use GPS or precise location
  • Basic device information required for app functionality

Guest users are not required to provide personal information to use the app. The app does not use the Android advertising ID (the permission is removed), and it has no chat or messaging between users.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to:

  • Authenticate your account
  • Save and restore game progress, coins and badges (also across devices for signed-in users)
  • Display quiz scores, leaderboards and weekly leagues
  • Show the weekly progress report and reminder notifications on the device
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain app security
  • Improve app performance and reliability

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Leaderboards and Weekly Leagues

Leaderboards and weekly leagues show other players only a first name or nickname, a country flag and points. Email addresses, photos and other details are never shown to other players. Players can join a league with a class code shared by a teacher or friend; this only groups players on one leaderboard. League entries are kept per week and old weeks may be deleted.

Child Profiles on a Shared Device

Brothers and sisters can each have their own profile on the same phone. A profile holds only a first name or nickname, a picture chosen from the app (an animal emoji), a class, and that child's progress. For guest play, profiles are stored on the device only. When a parent is signed in with Google, the list of children (first name or nickname, picture and class) is saved with that Google account so the same children appear on the family's other phones, and each child's scores are kept under their own player ID. A grown-up can remove a profile from a phone at any time; it is protected by a parent question.

Notifications, Reports and Certificates

The Android app can show a reminder notification and, on Sunday evenings, a weekly report of how many quizzes and games were finished that week. These notifications are created on the device. Certificates and score pictures are made on the device and are only shared if the user taps "Share".

Firebase Services

Our application uses Google Firebase Authentication to provide secure sign-in services, Firebase Realtime Database to store scores, progress, coins and leagues, and Firebase Analytics / Cloud Messaging to understand app usage and deliver notifications. Firebase may process your information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

For more information about Firebase Privacy:

https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Advertising

The application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Through AdMob mediation, ads may also be delivered by these ad networks, all of which take part in Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program:

  • Chartboost — privacy policy
  • Unity Ads — privacy policy
  • Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) — privacy policy
  • DT Exchange (Digital Turbine) — privacy policy

Because our app is intended for children, every ad request is marked as child-directed (COPPA) and for users under the age of consent, and ads are limited to a maximum content rating of "G" (general audiences). This means no personalised (interest-based) ads and no remarketing.

Google and these ad partners may collect limited technical information such as device type, operating system, IP address and app usage for ad delivery, ad measurement and fraud prevention. Our app does not give them the Android advertising ID.

Google Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children's Privacy

Our application is designed for children and families and follows Google Play's Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children. There is no chat, and other players only ever see a first name or nickname, a country flag and points.

Parents or legal guardians who believe their child has provided personal information that should be removed may contact us using the email address below.

Third-Party Services

This application may use the following services:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Analytics, Cloud Messaging)
  • Google Sign-In
  • Google Play Services (including in-app review and in-app updates)
  • Google AdMob and the mediation partners listed above (Chartboost, Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize, DT Exchange)

Each third-party service operates under its own privacy policy.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information. However, no method of transmission or storage over the Internet is completely secure.

Data Retention

Information associated with signed-in users is retained only as long as necessary to provide app functionality or until deletion is requested. Guest users may lose locally stored progress if the application is removed or app data is cleared.

Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Request deletion of your account data.
  • Ask questions about this Privacy Policy.
  • Report privacy concerns.
