Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Kids Quiz & Game ("App", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Android application.

Kids Quiz & Game is developed and published by Mayank Logic. This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application Kids Quiz & Game (com.mk.kidsquiz).

About Our App

Kids Quiz & Game is an educational application designed for children and families. The Android application securely displays content from our official website using WebView technology.

Login Options

Users may access the app using:

  • Guest Login
  • Google Sign-In

Guest Login allows users to play without signing in. If a user chooses Google Sign-In, Google Firebase Authentication is used to securely authenticate the user.

Information We Collect

Depending on how the app is used, we may collect:

  • Name (Google Sign-In only)
  • Email Address (Google Sign-In only)
  • Google Profile Picture (if available)
  • Firebase User ID (UID)
  • Game Progress
  • Quiz Scores
  • Basic Device Information required for app functionality

Guest users are not required to provide personal information to use the app.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to:

  • Authenticate your account
  • Save and restore game progress
  • Display quiz scores
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain app security
  • Improve app performance and reliability

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Firebase Services

Our application uses Google Firebase Authentication to provide secure sign-in services. Firebase may process your account information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

For more information about Firebase Privacy:

https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Advertising

The application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

For apps intended for children, we configure Google AdMob using child-appropriate settings, including a maximum ad content rating of "G" where applicable.

Google and its partners may collect limited technical information such as device identifiers, IP address, and advertising identifiers for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

Google Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy
