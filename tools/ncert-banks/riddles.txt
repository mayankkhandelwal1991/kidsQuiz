# RIDDLES — kid-friendly riddles, Class 3–5
## class3
I have hands but cannot clap. What am I? | A clock | A tree | A chair | A cup
I have a face and two hands but no arms or legs. What am I? | A clock | A doll | A mirror | A book
I have keys but cannot open locks. What am I? | A keyboard (or piano) | A door | A box | A car
What has to be broken before you can use it? | An egg | A pencil | A cup | A ball
I am full of holes but I can still hold water. What am I? | A sponge | A bucket | A cup | A bottle
What gets wetter the more it dries? | A towel | Water | The sun | A fan
I have a neck but no head. What am I? | A bottle | A giraffe | A shirt only | A pen
What has legs but cannot walk? | A table | A dog | A bird | A boy
What has teeth but cannot bite? | A comb | A lion | A dog | A shark
I go up and down but never move from my place. What am I? | Stairs | A ball | A bird | A car
What has an eye but cannot see? | A needle | A cat | A fish | A boy
What can you catch but not throw? | A cold | A ball | A stone | A bat
The more you take, the more you leave behind. What are they? | Footsteps | Sweets | Books | Toys
What falls but never gets hurt? | Rain | A glass | A boy | An egg
I am yellow and I shine in the sky in the day. What am I? | The Sun | The Moon | A lamp | A star at night
What has a bark but no bite? | A tree | A dog | A wolf | A cat
What has many pages but is not a tree? | A book | A leaf | A flower | A plant
What runs but never walks? | Water (a river) | A dog | A boy | A horse
What can fill a room but takes up no space? | Light | Water | Chairs | Toys
I have wheels and I take you to school. What am I? | A bus | A boat | A plane | A tree
## class4
What has a head and a tail but no body? | A coin | A snake | A fish | A dog
What goes up but never comes down? | Your age | A ball | A kite | Rain
What has cities but no houses, forests but no trees, and water but no fish? | A map | A book | A picture | A globe of glass
What word is spelled wrong in every dictionary? | Wrong | Right | Correct | Spelling
I am tall when I am young and short when I am old. What am I? | A candle | A tree | A person | A pencil only
What is always in front of you but cannot be seen? | The future | Your nose | A wall | The sky
What has one eye but cannot see? | A needle | A pirate | A cyclops | A potato
What can travel around the world while staying in a corner? | A stamp | A plane | A ship | A ball
What gets bigger the more you take away from it? | A hole | A cake | A pile of sand | A balloon
What can you hear but not see or touch? | Your voice (sound) | A cat | A book | Water
Which month has 28 days? | All of them | February | March | June
What has words but never speaks? | A book | A parrot | A radio | A teacher
What belongs to you but others use it more than you? | Your name | Your bag | Your shoes | Your pen
What has a ring but no finger? | A telephone | A hand | A bell tower | A glove
Forward I am heavy, backward I am not. What am I? | Ton | Car | Bag | Box
What breaks when you say its name? | Silence | Glass | A promise | A stick
What has four wheels and flies? | A garbage truck | A plane | A bird | A kite
What goes through a door but never goes in or out? | A keyhole | A key | A cat | A person
Which is heavier: a kilogram of cotton or a kilogram of iron? | Both weigh the same | Iron | Cotton | Cannot say
What can you keep after giving it to someone? | Your word (a promise) | A toy | Money | A book
## class5
A farmer has 15 sheep. All but 8 run away. How many are left? | 8 | 7 | 15 | 0
If you have me, you want to share me. If you share me, you don’t have me. What am I? | A secret | Food | Money | A toy
What has many keys but cannot open a single door? | A piano | A key ring | A house | A lock
What can run but never walks, has a mouth but never talks? | A river | A dog | A clock | A car
Mary’s father has five daughters: Nana, Nene, Nini, Nono. What is the fifth daughter’s name? | Mary | Nunu | Nina | Nanu
How many months have 30 days? | 11 (all except February) | 4 | 6 | 12
What comes once in a minute, twice in a moment, but never in a thousand years? | The letter M | The letter T | A second | The Sun
I am an odd number. Take away one letter and I become even. What am I? | Seven | Nine | Three | Five
What is so fragile that saying its name breaks it? | Silence | Glass | Ice | A bubble
What has a neck and no head, two arms but no hands? | A shirt | A bottle | A chair | A robot
The more there is, the less you see. What is it? | Darkness | Light | Water | Air
What can be cracked, made, told and played? | A joke | An egg | A game | A pot
A rooster lays an egg on top of a sloping roof. Which way does it roll? | Roosters don’t lay eggs | Left | Right | It stays
What is the next number: 2, 4, 6, 8, ? | 10 | 9 | 12 | 11
What is the next letter: A, C, E, G, ? | I | H | J | K
If two’s company and three’s a crowd, what are four and five? | Nine | A party | Seven | A team
What has 13 hearts but no other organs? | A deck of playing cards | A hospital | A monster | A book
What word becomes shorter when you add two letters to it? | Short | Long | Tall | Small
What starts with ‘e’, ends with ‘e’, and has only one letter in it? | An envelope | An eye | An eagle | An engine
I have branches but no fruit, trunk or leaves. What am I? | A bank | A tree | A river | A road
